# Business Requirements

## Business Problem

Hospital readmissions within 30 days can indicate opportunities to improve care transitions, follow-up planning, and resource allocation. This project will use historical hospital encounter data to understand readmission patterns and identify patient and encounter characteristics associated with higher readmission rates.

## Stakeholders

- Hospital clinical and operational leadership
- Care management and discharge-planning teams
- Quality-improvement teams
- Data and analytics teams

## Business Questions

- What proportion of eligible hospital encounters result in a readmission within 30 days?
- How do 30-day readmission rates vary across patient characteristics?
- How do 30-day readmission rates vary across encounter and utilization characteristics?
- Which characteristics are most strongly associated with higher readmission risk?
- How can the findings support monitoring, care-management prioritization, and future risk-analysis tools?

## Initial KPIs

- Number of eligible index encounters
- Number of 30-day readmissions
- Overall 30-day readmission rate
- 30-day readmission rate by selected patient characteristics
- 30-day readmission rate by selected encounter characteristics

Final KPI definitions, inclusion criteria, and reporting dimensions will be confirmed after the dataset and its documentation are reviewed.

## Expected Deliverables

- A documented and reproducible data-preparation workflow
- Exploratory analysis of 30-day readmission patterns
- SQL-based analysis in a local SQLite database
- A Tableau Public dashboard
- A validated readmission risk-analysis model, if supported by the available data
- A Streamlit risk-analysis application
- Project documentation and a portfolio-ready GitHub repository
